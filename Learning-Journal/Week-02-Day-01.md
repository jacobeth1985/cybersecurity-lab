# Week 2 – Day 1

## Topic

Linux Processes and Services

## Commands Practiced

- ps
- ps -ef
- ps aux
- top
- grep
- kill
- uptime
- systemctl
- journalctl

## What I Learned

Today I learned how to investigate running processes, monitor system resources, and understand Linux services.

I practiced finding processes, identifying their Process IDs (PIDs), terminating a process with the `kill` command, and confirming that it stopped successfully.

I also explored Linux services using `systemctl` and reviewed system logs using `journalctl`.

## Reflection

This lab helped me understand how SOC Analysts investigate suspicious processes, monitor system health, and use logs to identify security events. I also learned why collecting evidence before stopping a suspicious process is important during an incident response.
